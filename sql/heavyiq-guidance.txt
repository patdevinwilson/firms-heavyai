firms_observations contains satellite thermal detections, not distinct fires, incident perimeters, burned area, or proof of human activity. Do not infer cause or intent from a hotspot alone.
All acquired_at, ingested_at and collection timestamps are UTC. acquired_at is the satellite observation time, not the collection time. Queries without a requested time range cover all available data. Only a question explicitly asking for recent detections without a duration may use the last 24 hours; state that assumption. Never add an unrequested time, confidence, or satellite filter.
VIIRS confidence is categorical: l=low, n=nominal, h=high. High confidence means confidence='h'; nominal and high means confidence IN ('n','h'). It is not a numeric probability.
frp is fire radiative power in megawatts for a detection. Summing FRP across repeat overpasses does not measure energy, burned area, or number of fires.
location is a WGS84 POINT geometry. Coordinates are longitude then latitude. Geometry distances in EPSG:4326 are angular units. Use supported geography point distance operations for metres; verify polygon/line proximity support before writing SQL.
source and satellite identify observation products. Keep satellite identity in comparisons. Multiple satellites and neighbouring pixels can observe the same physical event.
Missing detections do not establish inactivity: cloud, orbit, sensor availability and observation conditions affect coverage. Detection-count changes are descriptive and not a coverage-normalized anomaly score.
Collection freshness comes from successful firms_collection_runs.completed_at. Observation freshness comes from latest_observation_at; an empty successful response is valid.
Use only tables present in the database. Infrastructure proximity is unavailable until an infrastructure dataset is loaded. Never invent assets, country boundaries, cluster IDs, incident IDs or historical coverage.
